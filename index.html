About me

I work in quantum computation and quantum information, especially quantum algorithms. My research has focused on efficient measurement algorithms for estimating observables in quantum many-body systems. I like watching schools of koi swim freely, so I let them swim across the background of this website. If they get in your way, you can adjust their number at the bottom of the page.

Research interests

  • What computational advantage can quantum computers offer for practical problems?
  • What are the limits of quantum information processing and of extracting information from quantum systems?
  • What mathematical structures determine the advantage of quantum algorithms?

These questions guide my research.
