2023

Physics Lab. 2023 — Quantum Physics Group

As the group leader, I coordinated its explanatory articles. The official group page collects the team's posters and articles. I wrote the poster “First Steps Toward Fault-Tolerant Quantum Computing” and gave a student lecture on this topic on 14 May 2023. On 6 December 2022, I also wrote the Physics Department Advent Calendar article “Drawing quantum circuits in LaTeX with Quantikz,” introducing gates, control lines, and measurements through code examples.

Physics Lab. 2022 — Quantum Physics Group

On 13 May 2022, I wrote the article “Quantum Support Vector Machines” for the group. It introduces support vector machines, kernel methods, and quantum computing basics, then explains classification with quantum feature maps and kernels.
