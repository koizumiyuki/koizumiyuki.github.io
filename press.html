  1. 14 May 2024

    What Should the Quantum Computers of the Future Aim At? — Toward Quantum Advantage with Practical Impact

    Joint press release by The University of Tokyo, NTT, and Osaka University. By comparing the computing time and resources required by advanced classical and quantum algorithms, the study identified conditions for quantum advantage in condensed matter problems and proposed practical targets for fault-tolerant quantum computers.

  2. 27 Feb 2026

    Faster Quantum Measurement Algorithm Enhanced by Symmetry — Toward the Practical Realization of Fault-Tolerant Quantum Simulation

    Joint press release by The University of Tokyo, Keio University, and Osaka University. The study developed a quantum algorithm that uses symmetry to estimate many observables with high precision and reduced computational cost, outperforming existing methods on representative condensed matter and quantum chemistry problems.
